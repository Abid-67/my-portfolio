About me

Hello! I am Kazi Abid Hasan, a graduate of the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), with a major in Electronics and Photonics.

My academic jorney has been driven by a deep passion for research and knowledge sharing. I have always enjoyed learinng new things, whether theoretical or hands-on lab or project works.

My undergraduate research focused on graphene-based Near-Field Thermophotovoltaic (TPV) systems, and I'm particularly interested in Optoelectronics, Photonics, Robotics, VLSI and Embedded Systems.

Outside the classroom, I have actively participated in extra curricular activities, holding leadership roles in IEEE BUET Student Branch and BUET Robotics Society. I served as the Vice-Chairperson of IEEE BUET Student Branch, organized and volunteered in several events. I also served as the Vice-President of BUET Robotics Society, where I organized workshops and mentored students in robotics and PCB designing, along with volunteering in several national events.

And apart from all these stuffs, I really love traveling.
